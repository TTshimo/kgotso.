Privacy

This site doesn't track you, sell anything, or ask you to sign up. Here's exactly what happens when you visit.

What Kgotso collects

Nothing. This site doesn't use cookies, analytics, or any form tracking your visit, your reading, or who you are. There's no account to create and nothing you're asked to submit.

What a third party sees

This site loads two typefaces from Google Fonts, so that it renders consistently on your device. To do that, your browser makes a request directly to Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address and basic request information for that font request, under Google's own privacy policy. That's the only outside service this site connects to.

Links to helplines and other organisations

The Get Help page links out to helplines, hospitals, and international directories. Once you follow one of those links or dial a number, you're interacting with that organisation directly, under their own privacy practices.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, or you want to know more about how this site is built, see the About page for a way to get in touch.
